02 · WebSEAL, Akamai & Junctions

The “front door” of ciam: how a request travels from the internet through Akamai EdgeSuite and WebSEAL, how WebSEAL decides who may pass (ACLs and POPs), how sessions are tracked, and how the /mga junction hands requests to the AAC/federation runtime.

1. Zero-knowledge explanation

📘 Beginner Concept — office building security

Akamai is the street-level guard: it blocks obviously bad visitors and only lets approved addresses reach certain doors. WebSEAL is the reception desk inside: it checks your badge (session cookie), looks up which rooms you're allowed into (ACLs), and escorts you down a corridor (a junction) to the right department. The department never checks your ID again — reception whispers who you are in a sealed note (HTTP headers iv-user, iv-creds).

  • Reverse proxy: a server that accepts requests on behalf of back-end servers. Clients never see the back end.
  • TLS termination: WebSEAL decrypts HTTPS using its certificate from a key database (pdsrv.kdb) and typically re-encrypts to the back end.
  • Junction: a mapping from a URL prefix (e.g. /mga) in WebSEAL's namespace to a back-end server. Everything under /mga/… is forwarded to the AAC/federation runtime.
  • Protected Object Space (POS): a tree of every URL WebSEAL can serve (/WebSEAL/<host>-default/mga/…). ACLs and POPs are attached to nodes of this tree.
  • ACL: who may do what on a node (e.g. unauthenticated may Traverse and read). POP: extra conditions (IP restrictions, time of day, quality of protection, audit level).
  • Session: after login WebSEAL sets the cookie PD-S-SESSION-ID (opaque handle to a server-side cache entry).

2. High-level architecture

🏗️ Architecture Deep Dive — two layers of access control
Layer 1 (Akamai) filters by network and request shape before traffic reaches the appliance; layer 2 (WebSEAL ACL/POP) filters by identity and object. A public endpoint such as /jwks must pass both. A 403 with “Reference #” from errors.edgesuite.net is Akamai; a 302 to a login page or a WebSEAL HTML error (e.g. HPDIA…) is WebSEAL.

3. Low-level technical sequence

Header detail
iv-user = the authenticated user name. iv-creds = a Base64 blob with the full credential (groups, extended attributes) that back ends can decode with the ISVA SDK. iv-groups, iv-remote-address and iv_jct are common companions. Which are injected depends on the junction flags (-c) — verify on your appliance with pdadmin … server task … show /mga.

⚠️ Security Pitfall — over-opening
Do not attach a permissive ACL at /mga itself or at /sps: ACLs inherit downward, so you would expose admin-ish endpoints. Attach unauthenticated Tr only to the specific /metadata and /jwks nodes (and to /authsvc and /authorize which must be reachable before login).
